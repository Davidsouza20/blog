What I’ve Learned#

Working as a software engineer has taught me a lot — not just about code, but about systems, teams, and tradeoffs.

Some of the most valuable lessons I’ve picked up:

  • Read the error message. Seriously, most answers are right there.
  • Boring technology is underrated. Proven tools exist for a reason.
  • Write it down. If it’s worth building, it’s worth documenting.
